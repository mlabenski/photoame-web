Frequently asked questions

How do I pair with someone?

One person creates an invite code in the app and shares it with the other, who enters it to pair. Each account can be paired with exactly one person at a time.

Why did my photo disappear?

That's how Photoame works. A snap is meant to be seen once (with a single replay within a short window) and is then deleted. Photos never save to your camera roll.

How do I keep a photo?

Tap Keep while viewing it. A photo is saved to your shared "Us" gallery only when both of you choose to keep it.

How do I report or block someone?

You can report or block your partner from Settings, or directly while viewing a snap using the ••• menu. Blocking ends your pair, deletes shared snaps, and permanently prevents re-pairing. Reports go to our team for review.

How do I end a pair?

Go to Settings → End pair. This deletes all snaps shared between you and your partner.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all associated data and cannot be undone.

I'm not getting notifications

Check that notifications are enabled for Photoame in iOS Settings → Notifications, and that you have a network connection.
