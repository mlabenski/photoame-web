A private space for two.

Photoame is a photo-messaging app for exactly one other person. Send a photo, it's seen once, then it's gone — unless you both choose to keep it.

Privacy policy Support

Just the two of you

Each account pairs with one person. No feeds, no followers, no strangers.

Made to disappear

Photos are viewed once, with a single replay, then deleted. They never touch your camera roll.

Kept only together

A photo is saved only when both of you tap Keep. Otherwise it's gone for good.

Yours to control

Report or block from anywhere, and delete your account and all your data at any time.
